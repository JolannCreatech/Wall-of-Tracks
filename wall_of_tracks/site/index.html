    The small print

    A wall that listens.

    Wall of Tracks turns the last 200 songs on your Last.fm profile into a pocket-sized gallery. The iPod is both the exhibition and the remote: spin the wheel to hang a different record on the wall.

    Controls

    Drag or scroll on the wheel · ↑↓ to browse · Enter to select · Esc to go back.

    How it works

    The browser only speaks to this little server. The server keeps the Last.fm key, caches recent tracks, and quietly proxies every sleeve image.

    Album covers and listening data belong to their artists and to you. Data courtesy of Last.fm.
